# Open the ER diagram

1. Open a `.dbml` file.
2. Click the diagram button in the editor title bar, or run DBML Viewer: Open ER Diagram from the Command Palette.

The diagram opens beside the editor and follows your edits as you type. Problems in the file show in the Problems panel with their line, and the diagram keeps drawing what it could read.

The outline lists tables, columns, enums and groups. In a `Ref` line, Ctrl or Cmd + click a `table.column` to jump to it.
